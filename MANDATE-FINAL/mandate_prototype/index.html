Private markets · deal intelligence

A clearer path from
opportunity to capital.

Mandate is a concept for making private-market fundraising more organized, research-led, and relevant. It brings deal preparation, investor criteria, and market context into one considered workflow.

The core idea

Match the opportunity to the right capital partner.

Mandate helps deal teams move from a company or transaction profile to a more relevant investor shortlist. It compares the opportunity with investor mandates, then makes the reasons for each potential fit easier to review before outreach.

01
Describe the deal

Sector, geography, capital sought, transaction type and key financials.

02
Compare investor mandates

Check size, preferred structures, sectors, regions and stage of investment.

03
Prioritize with context

Review fit drivers, missing information and diligence points before making an introduction.

Build a comps and research checklist

This creates a structured research workplan and links to live market-data searches. It does not invent trading multiples or pull a live price feed.
Start with a company or sector.Mandate will outline relevant metrics, diligence questions and source searches.
Trading comparables
Revenue and EBITDA growth, gross margin, EBITDA margin, EV/revenue, EV/EBITDA, P/E, free-cash-flow yield and balance-sheet quality. Use consistent LTM / NTM periods.
Operating drivers
Separate price, volume, mix, churn, utilization, labor, capex and working capital. Distinguish reported performance from one-time adjustments.
Private-market bridge
Adjust for scale, growth, customer concentration, liquidity, control, leverage, governance, cyclicality and sponsor value-creation assumptions.

Product guide

Clear inputs. Explainable outputs.

Mandate is designed to help a deal professional make a better first pass, not to replace underwriting, investor verification or legal review.

Deal matching workflow

  1. Upload a one-pager, presentation, Word document or paste text.
  2. Review and correct the extracted deal profile.
  3. Compare against investor sector, geography, capital type and check-size preferences.
  4. Review fit score, match reasons and missing criteria.
  5. Draft outreach, then add the investor to your pipeline.

Investment memo workflow

  1. Upload a .pptx pitch deck or paste slide text.
  2. Extract source facts and organize the company narrative.
  3. Generate a structured memo with thesis, business overview, financials, market, risks and diligence questions.
  4. Review every figure against the original deck; correct errors and fill gaps.
  5. Download the memo draft and route it through your internal review.

What this prototype does and doesn't do

Works in this demo
Sample teasers, text input, browser-side text extraction for supported files, illustrative ranked leads, pipeline tracking and memo export.
Not connected yet
Live investor/contact databases, email sending, external CRM sync, server-side AI, access controls, secure storage and real investor verification.
Production requirements
Secure document processing, citations to source slides/pages, financial validation, permissioned investor data and legal review of capital-raising workflows.
