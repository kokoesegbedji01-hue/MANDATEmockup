OVERVIEW
Northstar Packaging Solutions (“Northstar”) is a fictional founder-owned specialty packaging manufacturer headquartered in Columbus, Ohio, with production facilities in Ohio and Georgia. It produces recyclable rigid packaging, short-run custom formats and secondary packaging for food, personal care and healthcare customers. Northstar combines recurring B2B relationships with custom tooling and shorter production runs for customers that are too small for large-scale packaging suppliers.

FY2025 FINANCIAL PROFILE (ALL FIGURES INVENTED)
Revenue: $28.0 million | EBITDA: $4.2 million | EBITDA margin: 15.0% | Revenue growth: 14% year over year | Repeat-order revenue: approximately 60% | Capacity utilization: approximately 78%. Figures are illustrative, unaudited and not based on a real company.

TRANSACTION
Northstar is seeking $12.0 million of growth equity to install a new production line, fund incremental working capital and evaluate a regional add-on acquisition. The fictional owner prefers a minority partner with flexible governance and relevant industrial or packaging experience. No valuation, ownership percentage, investor return or definitive transaction terms have been established.

ILLUSTRATIVE USE OF PROCEEDS
$5.0M new production line and installation; $2.5M working capital and inventory; $2.0M potential add-on acquisition; $1.0M automation and quality systems; $1.5M fees, contingency and general corporate purposes. These allocations are illustrative planning assumptions only.

INVESTMENT THESIS TO TEST
1. Additional production capacity may support growth if demand and utilization materialize. 2. Repeat B2B relationships and custom tooling may support customer retention. 3. A regional add-on acquisition could broaden customer access and purchasing scale. Each point requires independent evidence; none is an established fact about a real business.

KEY RISKS AND DILIGENCE QUESTIONS
Customer concentration and contract duration; resin and other input-price volatility; ability to pass through cost increases; maintenance versus growth capex; utilization and scrap rates; environmental and workplace compliance; quality of earnings; customer retention and win/loss history; management depth; acquisition pipeline and purchase price; working-capital seasonality; debt and lien status; and valuation / governance terms.
